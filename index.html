FlatPDF

Turn every page of a PDF into a single flat image. Text, layers, form fields and annotations become pixels that can no longer be selected, edited or lifted out.

Everything runs in this tab. Your files never leave your device.

Output
Leaves out everything the PDF marks as screen-only, the way a printer would. Comments, highlights or filled fields that were not marked for printing will be missing.
OCR protection
Bends the page slightly and scatters small marks over the text so text recognition software misreads it. It lowers what OCR gets right; it cannot make text unreadable to software while a person can still read it.
